I worked on leadership when we had to make posters, and my leadership teacher made me the leader of my group of 6 people. She had extremely strict expectations that she wanted us to meet with the poster because she likes everything to be perfect, and at the end of the day, my group and I ended up finishing all of our posters and finished with a day to spare that we used to hang up our posters, and spread around to see if anyone else wanted some of our help finishing up theirs. Overall, being the leader taught me how to be a leader, communication is key, all ideas matter, no arguing, and to always be efficient.
