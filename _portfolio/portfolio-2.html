--- title: "News Article" excerpt: "Create a News Article
" collection: portfolio --- I worked on writing a news article about a fellow classmate. It took me three days: two days to gather the information by interviewing them, and one day to write the article and find the picture that I felt properly demonstrated. Some tools used to write a news article were a notebook, a pen, Canva, a computer, and a very reliable source of information. I learned about the person I interviewed. I learned her goals, what she wants to do with her life after high school. The challenge solved was getting photos to go into the article.
